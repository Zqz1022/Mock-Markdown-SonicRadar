## Product Description

The **SR-9000 Industrial Sonic Radar** is AcuScan Industrial AG's flagship long-range acoustic detection platform, engineered for deployment in extreme industrial and geophysical environments.

Unlike conventional ultrasonic proximity sensors, the SR-9000 employs **phased-array acoustic beam steering** to achieve:

- Up to **320 metres** detection range in open air
- Up to **85 metres** through dense solid media (rock, soil, reinforced concrete)

### Key Application Areas

- **Seismic & Geological Survey** — Long-range acoustic profiling for hazard assessment and formation analysis
- **Underwater & Marine Detection** — Subsurface obstacle mapping, harbour monitoring, pipeline inspection
- **Industrial Perimeter Security** — 360° acoustic surveillance for refineries, power plants, chemical facilities
- **Subterranean Mapping** — Non-invasive underground void detection and tunnel inspection
- **Offshore & Energy Platforms** — Vibration-isolated sensing for offshore rigs and subsea pipelines
- **Research & Laboratory** — High-precision acoustic measurement for materials science and structural testing

### Technical Highlights

- Core sensor: piezoelectric composite crystal array
- Frequency range: 20 Hz – 80 kHz (ultra-wideband)
- DSP: real-time noise filtering, azimuth accuracy ±0.05°
- Interfaces: Ethernet, RS-485, CAN bus
- Enclosure: IP67, ATEX Zone 2, vibration-isolated mount
